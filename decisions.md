# Margem Cool — decisions

Why we chose what we chose, so it isn't re-litigated.

## Portuguese first, against the portfolio rule
**2026-08-10.** Every other brand drafts in English for James to review, then translates. Here the demand is measured: `margem sul` draws 4,400 searches a month while `costa da caparica` draws 201,000 and `almada` 135,000, essentially all Portuguese-language on google.pt. Writing English first is how Cacilhas and Belverde ended up fully written in English with placeholder Portuguese — two flagship pages ranking in the wrong language. English editions are translations.

## The brand is Margem Cool; the keywords are place names
**2026-08-10.** The keyword universe was built on "X na Margem Sul" phrasings — *best neighbourhoods margem sul*, *festas e feiras margem sul*. Nearly all return zero volume. Target place names instead.

## Complete coverage is the product, not a traffic play
**2026-08-10.** Every place page gets written, including freguesias with no measurable demand. You cannot be the go-to resource for a region with eight pages. Volume sequences the work; it does not decide whether to do it.

## Guides are compiled, without visit markers
**2026-08-10.** No "we've been here" badge, and equally no claimed visit. Recommendations rest on observable grounds — position, queue, walking distance, what a kitchen is known for — never on a meal we did not eat.

## Publish first, review after
**2026-08-10.** Portuguese is never held as `draft: true` awaiting Lucia. She reviews what is live; corrections are applied forward.

## A page earns indexing by having content
**2026-08-10.** Placeholders and unwritten pillars are `noindex, follow` and out of the sitemap. Submitting ~130 URLs of which 98% were stubs is the textbook thin-content signal, arriving while Google forms a view of a new domain. Both mechanisms read one rule and the build fails if they disagree.

## Unwritten pages help rather than apologise
**2026-08-10.** No "Em construção". An unwritten place page lists the guides that cover it. Standing portfolio rule: build it or leave it off.

## Real maps, never hand-drawn
Leaflet with CARTO tiles and real GeoJSON. Hand-tracing coordinates into an SVG turns every geographic detail into a manual problem and the fix loop never ends.

## Keeping facts true comes before writing more
**2026-08-10.** Perishable claims declare their source in frontmatter and are re-checked weekly. The Transpraia beach train stopped running in 2019 and guides across the internet still recommend it; that is the failure mode this exists to prevent.
