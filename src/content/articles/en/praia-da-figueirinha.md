Figueirinha is the most sought-after beach in the Arrábida and the easiest of them all — and those are the same fact.

It sits inside the Parque Natural da Arrábida, facing south into the bay of Setúbal, with the hills cutting the Atlantic swell behind it. The result is a large beach with wide sand and water that comes in very gradually: you can walk a long way out and still be waist-deep.

Across the bay you can see the **Tróia sandspit**. At low tide a sandbank forms and reaches out into the water, changing the shape of the beach by the hour.

## The car doesn't get through

This is the fact that decides the day. From **4 June to 15 September**, every day, **7am to 8pm**, private cars are barred — between Travessa da Casa da Saúde, by the Saboaria, and the beach.

The Figueirinha car park is reserved for concession staff, motorcycles and vehicles with a reduced-mobility permit.

Outside that period, and outside those hours, you drive down normally.

## Getting there

**Bus 4474**, Alegro to Figueirinha. In July and August it runs an intensive timetable, dozens of trips a day each way, departing from both Alegro and the Várzea terminal. Covered by the **navegante pass**.

It is the practical way to arrive in summer, and considerably faster than hunting for parking that isn't there.

## Who it's for

**Families with small children.** That is why Figueirinha is what it is: shallow water, no swell, wide sand, and a concession with services.

Anyone after a small cove and deep water should carry on to **Galapos and Galapinhos**, further west, or to the **Portinho da Arrábida**. The [Arrábida beaches guide](/en/guides/praias-da-arrabida/) covers them all.

## The honest part

Figueirinha is the busiest beach in the hills and it shows. In August it fills early, and the traffic restriction exists precisely because, before it, summer days were a queue of cars parked on top of a protected area.

September, after the 15th, is the best moment of the year: water still warm, the road open again, and a fraction of the people.
