# Margem Cool — brand guidelines

The single source for identity. The full canon lives in [`docs/standards/brand.md`](../docs/standards/brand.md); this is the summary the Spandera standard expects.

## Thesis
A useful information site about the Margem Sul, written from inside it. Not a tourism brochure and not a publication aspiring to be an institution — a resource that answers the question someone actually typed, for the people who live on this side of the river.

## Audience
Residents of the nine concelhos first. Then newcomers and people moving here, then day-trippers and visitors. If a page is useful only to a visitor, it is in the wrong place.

## Values
**Useful before impressive.** **Honest before flattering.** **Specific before atmospheric.** The name is a promise: if we use it, the pages have to be worth looking at.

## Voice
Plain, direct, quietly opinionated where there is genuine signal. Portuguese in the *tu* register, verb conjugation carrying it. Portuguese terms used freely in English and never italicised. Place names keep their diacritics in both languages.

**Never:** joia escondida, segredo bem guardado, imperdível, encantador, pitoresco, autêntico — or in English, hidden gem, must-visit, stunning, charming, authentic, perfect for. No exclamation marks. No source named in the prose. No claimed visit. No invented ranking.

**Always:** "in the Margem Sul", never "on the Margem Sul".

## Look
Palette **Industrial Coast** — teal `#0F5C5D`, rust `#B85C38`, cream `#F5F1E8`, charcoal `#1F2328`, cool grey `#8A949E`. Type: Outfit Variable for display and small caps, Source Serif 4 for body and headings. The river curve from the wordmark recurs as a layout element. A change of background tone means a different *kind* of content, never a subsection.

## Naming
Margem Cool. The publication credits its own photographs as Margem Cool — never a personal name.
