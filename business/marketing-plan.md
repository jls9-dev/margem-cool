# Margem Cool — Marketing Plan

**Internal only.** How Margem Cool wins a local audience and (later) revenue. Companion to

assumptions, don't invent]**.

## 1. Who we're talking to
Residents and newcomers to the **Margem Sul** (Lisbon's south bank) first — people living there or
moving there who want to know what's on and where to go — plus day-trippers and tourists. A
fast-growing, under-documented area (Lisbon's overflow across the river): little good local info
online, so a first-mover can own the search.

## 2. Positioning & message
The **useful, characterful, residents-first** guide to the Margem Sul. The name is a promise: it must
**actually be cool** — visually interesting, genuinely helpful, never corporate. Always **"in the
Margem Sul"**.

## 3. Channels (SEO + automation lead everything)
- **Owned/SEO content** — what's on, where to go, eating/drinking, beaches, getting around, living
  locally; ranking for resident and visitor questions.
- **Automation is a core operating principle** — engineered for weekly auto-publish with minimal input;
  tiered so the brand voice is protected on the pieces that matter.
- **Feeds South Bank Real Estate** — the warm local audience is also the property funnel's top.

## 4. Funnel — visitor → value (then revenue)
Search → useful guide → **return visits / owned audience** → (later) revenue via **local business
listings/sponsorship, affiliate and events**, plus property leads passed to South Bank. Monetisation is
**future**; build audience first. **Timing/mix [fill]**.

## 5. Content & SEO engine
Pillars: what's on, go, eat/drink, beaches & outdoors, practical/living. Largely auto-drafted then
human-reviewed (James edits EN, Lucia reviews PT). Useful-for-residents is the north star.

## 6. Measurement
Sessions · returning visitors · top pages · email/subscribers · leads passed to South Bank. Targets
**[fill]**. Monthly review.

## 7. Cadence & ownership
Weekly: auto-publish + light review. Monthly: review + focus. Maximally automated by design.
