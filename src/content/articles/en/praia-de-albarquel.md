Albarquel is the beach Setúbal has at its door. It sits at the western end of the waterfront, where the hill road begins, already inside the Parque Natural da Arrábida — and it is the only beach in these hills you can reach **on foot from a city centre**.

That is the whole argument. It is not the most beautiful beach in the Arrábida and it does not try to be.

## What makes it useful

**You get there without a car.** On foot along the waterfront, or on the **4471** circular. In hills where access is the defining problem of summer, that solves a great deal.

**There is a park alongside.** The **Parque Urbano de Albarquel** gives shade, paths and a terrace, which makes it a beach for an afternoon without a plan — you don't need the whole day organised in advance.

**The water is calm.** It sits inside the bay, sheltered from the Atlantic swell by the hills.

## What it isn't

It does not have the clarity of Galapos, Galapinhos or the Portinho. It is closer to the city and the port, and you can see that in the water.

Anyone after the blue of the Arrábida photographs has to go further west — and, in summer, take the bus to get there. The [Arrábida beaches guide](/en/guides/praias-da-arrabida/) explains how.

## Summer access

From **4 June to 15 September**, **7am to 8pm**, private cars are restricted from Travessa da Casa da Saúde, by the Saboaria — the same eastern zone that serves Figueirinha.

Because Albarquel is walkable from the centre, the restriction bites less here than anywhere else in the hills.

## The honest part

Albarquel is an urban beach, with everything that implies: more people from the city, less silence, and a view that includes the working port to the east.

In exchange it gives the thing no other Arrábida beach gives: you can finish lunch in central Setúbal and be on the sand twenty minutes later, with no car and no plan.
