Galapinhos is the Arrábida's small cove — wedged between limestone cliffs, with water that gets deep quickly and holds the clarity that gives the beach its reputation.

It lies west of Figueirinha, on the stretch of hills that also holds Galapos, Creiro and the Portinho.

## Galapos and Galapinhos are not the same beach

They get confused constantly. They are two adjoining coves separated by rock: **Galapos** is larger and has a beach concession; **Galapinhos** is the smaller and more secluded, reached by path.

If someone tells you they parked at Galapinhos, they were somewhere else.

## Getting there

From **4 June to 15 September**, **7am to 8pm**, access to this side of the hills is restricted from the **cruzamento do Pinheiro** on the ER379-1.

In practice: drive to the **Creiro car park** — around 140 spaces, **€5 before 1pm and €3 after** — and from there take the **free 4477 shuttle** to Galapos and walk on. Or take the trail from Creiro.

When the Creiro car park fills, the road closes. On August weekends that happens in the morning.

## What to expect

Limited sand, deep water within a few metres, rock on both sides. There are effectively no facilities — bring water.

The beach turned up on best-of lists and the effect was predictable: it is unchanged, the quiet is not. At midday in August it is small for the number of people it receives.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants clear water and doesn't mind walking to reach it. For swimming, for diving off the rock, and for staying put.

It is not the beach for small children — for that, [Figueirinha](/en/guides/praia-da-figueirinha/) has wide sand, shallow water and a direct bus.

## The honest part

What makes Galapinhos good is exactly what makes it difficult: it is small and it is far. The day there is a road and a car park at the door, it stops being worth the trip.

Go out of season, or go very early. In September, after the 15th, the restriction lifts and the water is at its warmest point of the year.
